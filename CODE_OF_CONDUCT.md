# Contributor Code of Conduct

Everyone contributing to SMIUNotes is expected to communicate respectfully and collaborate constructively regardless of background, experience or identity.

Be welcoming to new learners. Critique changes and decisions, not people. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish another person's private information or pressure anyone to share credentials. Respect students' privacy and the project's independent, unofficial status.

Report concerning behavior privately to the repository maintainers using an authorized contact channel. Maintainers may edit/remove comments, restrict participation or decline contributions to protect community safety. Decisions should be proportionate and made fairly. This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests and project-managed community spaces.
